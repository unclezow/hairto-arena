# Lobby currency icons

## NEAR

`near-token.png` is the official NEAR logo supplied by the developer from the
NEAR brand kit. Its pixels are preserved. It identifies the wallet's NEAR gas
balance; the mark and branding belong to NEAR.

## USDC (mock)

`usdc-coin-v1.png` preserves the original transparent dollar-coin illustration.
The generic dollar symbol, gold rim and emerald enamel identify the testnet
USDC balance. It is decorative artwork rather than an official USDC logo.
Balances come from the configured NEAR account and fungible-token contract.

The original image was generated with the built-in image generation tool. Its
central emblem was edited to a raised gold dollar symbol while preserving the
coin silhouette, materials, lighting, perspective and transparent background.

## Earlier concept

`duelvault-token-v1.png` is retained as unused original concept artwork: a gold
coin with an emerald center and a shield-shaped D. It does not represent a
deployed token and is not used for current balances.
